Thank you for your interest in Nanum Learn!

Nanum Learn provides numerous resources like Nanums, handouts, and
books (collectively, "Services"). Our mission is to make our resources
more accessible to the public.

## Our Content and License

Most of our Services are released under the CC BY 4.0 license.
Meaning, if you are interested in using our works, you do not
need to request any permission from us given that you mention that
the work is from Nanum Learn. For more information, please visit
the [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Some of our contents like LibreNotebook are released under different,
a slightly more restrictive, license. Please review respective
license before using our Services!

Despite our efforts, the resources that we provide may include
accidental inaccuracies, and are provided as is. For more information
on contributing, please visit
[Contributing](https://nanumlearn.org/contribute).

## Comments and Nanums

Any comments left in our website is copyrighted by the respective
writers. However by posting, you let Nanum Learn to store, display,
and delete the comments. We moderate the comments through GitHub
Discussions to prevent any abuse to our system. Although the comments
are designed to be anonymous, we have the right to delete any abusive
comments such as slurs and discriminative comments. Please be
thoughtful and respectful when leaving comments. The same policy
applies for Nanums as well.
